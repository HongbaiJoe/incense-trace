# Cycle 2 evidence-map flow

This is a bounded, reproducible evidence map—not a PRISMA systematic review. Database-wide hit counts and a complete deduplication denominator were not retained during the seed search, so a PRISMA diagram would imply a search completeness that the present record cannot support.

- Method papers retained after topic and methods checking: 11
- Primary analytical sources retained after statement-level evidence checking: 17
- Explicit exclusions retained: 2
- Total included records in the Cycle 2 evidence matrix: 28
- Records with verified analytical-source metadata: 17

A submission-grade systematic review requires fresh, frozen searches in at least three comprehensive indexes, complete hit counts, deduplication IDs, title/abstract decisions, full-text exclusion reasons and backward/forward citation tracking. Institutional Web of Science, Scopus and CNKI coverage remains an external gate rather than being fabricated here.
