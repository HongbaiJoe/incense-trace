# Venue target

Checked: 2026-09-08

## Primary venue

**npj Heritage Science, Article**

Fit decision: strong. The journal explicitly accepts original heritage research based on computation and lists artificial intelligence, expert systems, digital humanities, digital textual analysis and intangible heritage within scope. Xiangji contributes a provenance-preserving computational benchmark and a source-shifted probabilistic model for historical incense formulas.

Official requirements applied to the submission candidate:

- title no longer than 15 words, without punctuation, idiom or pun;
- unstructured abstract no longer than 150 words;
- Introduction without subheadings;
- Results with subheadings;
- Discussion without subheadings and without separate limitations or conclusion sections;
- Methods with subheadings;
- mandatory Data availability, Code availability, Author contributions and Competing interests;
- about 60 references as a soft limit;
- one file per multi-panel figure, lowercase `a`, `b`, `c` panel labels;
- figure legends no longer than 350 words;
- a single Supplementary Information file;
- format-flexible initial submission in Word or PDF, with figures preferably embedded near their first citation.

Official pages:

- Scope: https://www.nature.com/npjheritagesci/aims
- Author instructions: https://www.nature.com/npjheritagesci/for-authors-and-referees/submisions
- Content types: https://www.nature.com/npjheritagesci/for-authors-and-referees/about/content-types

## Backup venue

**Digital Applications in Archaeology and Cultural Heritage, Research Article**

Fit decision: usable if the primary venue rejects on breadth. DAACH publishes innovative digital methods and models for cultural heritage and values preserved, accessible digital research objects. The backup version would shorten the heritage-science framing and foreground benchmark construction, source shift, structured prediction and software release.

Official journal page: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/digital-applications-in-archaeology-and-cultural-heritage

## Positioning decision

The paper uses “formula completion” for the evaluated task and reserves “reconstruction” for the broader heritage objective. It does not claim sensory reproduction. This distinction separates the work from GC-MS/O reconstruction of a surviving smell residue and from descriptor-to-mixture scent design.
