# V2 figure contract

## Figure 1

Core conclusion: Formula completion is evaluated as nested transfer across six historical work groups, with every graph and hyperparameter estimate rebuilt without the target work.
Figure archetype: Schematic-led composite.
Target journal/output: General computer science and cheminformatics manuscript; vector PDF and SVG plus 600 dpi TIFF and 300 dpi PNG preview.
Backend: Python with Matplotlib.
Final size: 183 mm by 102 mm.
Panel map: a, task definition from observed to hidden ingredients; b, nested leave-one-work-out design; c, source-stable conditional-lift calculation.
Evidence hierarchy: The nested source split is the hero evidence. The task and score construction explain what is estimated.
Statistics needed: Six outer work folds, five deterministic masks, 50% primary observation fraction.
Source data needed: Curated corpus metadata and the mathematical specification.
Image-integrity notes: Diagram contains no photographic or microscopy data.
Reviewer risk: A reader could mistake formula-level repetitions for independent samples; the panel explicitly marks the historical work as the inferential unit.

## Figure 2

Core conclusion: Conditional graph methods improve hidden-ingredient F1 over global frequency across five of six held-out works, but SSCL does not improve materially over the direct graph.
Figure archetype: Quantitative grid.
Target journal/output: General computer science and cheminformatics manuscript; vector PDF and SVG plus 600 dpi TIFF and 300 dpi PNG preview.
Backend: Python with Matplotlib.
Final size: 183 mm by 93 mm.
Panel map: a, source-macro hidden F1 and weighted Jaccard for six models; b, work-level hidden F1 for frequency, directed graph, and SSCL; c, paired F1 differences from frequency.
Evidence hierarchy: Work-level results are primary. Macro averages summarize them. Paired differences show the independent-block pattern.

Image-integrity notes: All marks are generated directly from experiment JSON.
Reviewer risk: The 0.0003 SSCL advantage could be overstated; the figure directly displays the near overlap and states the paired comparison.
